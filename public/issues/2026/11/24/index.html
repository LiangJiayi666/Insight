核对摘录：Nvidia’s board undermined its claims of independence by reinstating CEO Jensen Huang before investigations into his conduct concluded, investors and corporate governance experts said. The decision is striking because the board effectively vouched for Huang’s candor and fitness to lead the company before its own investigation was complete, raising doubts about whether directors had sufficient information to make an independent judgment, these people said. Nvidia said on Monday that after talks with senior US officials, its board "is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk." The abrupt U-turn, just days after the board vowed to look elsewhere for a successor, has also drawn US President Donald Trump and his administration into Nvidia’s corporate governance. Nvidia shares closed 31.5% higher on Monday even as it was becoming clear that the chipmaker’s board acted under pressure from Trump and others, and the president took credit for the change. The company said an independent board committee is still investigating reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort, and said "the committee’s investigation will proceed to conclusion," with Huang’s cooperation.

核对摘录：TOKYO, Nov 24 (Reuters) - The Japanese government downgraded its view on consumer spending in November for the first time in more than 2-1/2 years as rising prices weighed on consumer sentiment. On private consumption, the government cut its view on consumer spending for the first time since February 2024, saying it "appears to be pausing for picking up." In October, it said spending was "showing movements of picking up." On exports, the government upgraded its view for the first time since June, to "showing movements of picking up," as shipments to the US and EU picked up. On industrial production, authorities raised their assessment for the first time since September, saying it was "picking up as a whole."
